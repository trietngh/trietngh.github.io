## Data Visualization
Using D3.js, we plotted hundreds of thousands of data points received directly from the Mars rover telemetry systems. 

## Technical Challenges
Handling such a large dataset in the browser required specific optimizations to React's rendering lifecycle to avoid freezing the UI.
